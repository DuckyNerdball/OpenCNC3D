# G304. A REMASTERED STRUCTURE GOES UP PART BY PART, AS THE LAB'S PLACE PLAYS IT.
#
# Under Enhanced a structure put down (game/ebld_place.h, game/eplc/<TYPE>.eplc) rises out
# of the ground slab first, then part by part, with the dust of every part passing through
# the slab, the game's placement jolt and a rumble as the main structure rises, and at the
# end it is exactly the remastered model at rest. This script stages it four ways on
# SCG90EA and dumps the system's own state; gates.sh runs it four times (Enhanced twice,
# Enhanced with --noeplc, and Classic) and reads the dumps.
#
# 1. THE REAL THING: a Power Plant built from the sidebar and put down south of the
#    Construction Yard (cell 52,53, out of reach of the enemy, so no hit cuts it short),
#    dumped and shot at the press, 0.4 s (the slab's phase), 1.2 and 1.6 s (the main
#    structure rising: the rumble), 2.2 s, its end, and six seconds after the end (the dust
#    and the ring long gone: the hand-over frame).
# 2. THE WEAPONS FACTORY'S PLACEMENT PLAYED AGAIN (eplcplay, the lab's Place button) and
#    dumped tick by tick across its end, where its two roof fans hand over from their spin
#    up to the idle turn.
# 3. THE CONSTRUCTION YARD'S, whose one jolt is the placement's own, 1.724 s in, as its
#    MCV's box lands on the slab: the camera's state read on the ticks round it.
# 4. THE WEAPONS FACTORY SOLD: the placement backwards on the engine's own sell clock.
#
# The ECHO lines name each dump for the suite; gates.sh rewrites the shot names per run.
