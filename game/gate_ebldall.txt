# G274. EVERY REMASTERED STRUCTURE AND WALL LOADS, AND THE INTACT-ONLY ONES DRAW AND MOVE.
#
# Under Enhanced every approved remastered structure is drawn from its ebld file
# (game/ebld_mod.h): the Power Plant with its full wear-down and wreck, every other one
# from its intact model alone, with its moving parts moving, and the sandbag, chain-link
# and concrete walls from their sixteen connection pieces. gates.sh runs this script four
# times (Enhanced twice, Enhanced with --noebld, and Classic) and reads the dumps.
#
# THE SCENE is SCG90EA's two bases. The camera first sits over the player's Construction
# Yard (cell 53,51: intact only, two turning fans), twice, three ticks apart, so the fans'
# drawn angle can be read off each dump; then over the Nod base's Gun Turrets and the
# sandbag line in front of them (cell 50,36 and 57,36, wall cells on row 37). Each ebldump
# reports what the frame BEFORE it drew (the observer counts are cleared by the dump), so
# every dump follows a shot. Then the Construction Yard is killed with two hurtobj (800 to
# 50, then dead): an intact-only building leaves a wreck with no pieces and no old
# shatter. The ECHO lines name the dumps.
tick 20
cam 54 50
zoom max
shot shots/g274_a.png
echo g274 first
obj FACT 0
ebldump
tick 3
shot shots/g274_b.png
echo g274 second
ebldump
cam 53 36
shot shots/g274_c.png
echo g274 guns
obj GUN 0
obj GUN 1
ebldump
cam 54 50
hurtobj FACT 0
tick 20
hurtobj FACT 0
tick 30
shot shots/g274_d.png
echo g274 dead
ebldump
quit
